Frequently asked questions

Am I too old to learn piano?

No. The adult brain keeps its motor-learning plasticity; a 2016 PLoS One study (PMC4911097) found measurable cortical change after just 10 days of piano training in adults. Learners in their 60s and 70s regularly reach enjoyable playing. See our page on age.

How long does it take to learn piano as an adult?

With 15 to 20 minutes of daily practice, most adults play simple pieces within a few months and become comfortable within one to two years. Consistency predicts progress more than talent or age.

Can I learn piano with just an app?

For casual goals, often yes. Apps excel at daily practice, reading and motivation but can't see your posture and technique. The strongest setup is an app for daily practice plus an occasional teacher.

What is the best piano app for adult beginners?

It depends on your goal: flowkey or Simply Piano for songs, Pianote for instructor-led video, and a separate reading trainer for fundamentals. Many adults pair a song app with a fundamentals tool. See our 2026 app comparison.

Do I need an 88-key weighted piano, or is a 61-key fine?

For serious piano learning, buy 88 weighted keys, the Roland FP-10 and Yamaha P-145 are standard entry choices. A 61-key unweighted keyboard is fine for casual or synth use but you'll out-grow it.

How much should I practice each day?

15 to 20 focused minutes daily is ideal at the start and beats occasional long sessions. Habit research (UCL, 66-day median to automaticity) shows frequency matters more than duration for making practice stick.

Why can't I read sheet music even after months of apps?

Most song apps let you pass lessons by copying an on-screen guide, so you never truly read. Reading is a recall skill that needs direct, timed, spaced-repetition drilling, see our sight-reading page.

Is a digital piano good enough to learn on?

Yes. A weighted 88-key digital piano is more than adequate, adds silent practice with headphones and app connectivity, and costs less than an acoustic. Many players use one for years.
