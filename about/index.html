About this site

About Learn Piano Brief

Updated August 2026 · Independent review · Cites primary sources

Learn Piano Brief is an independent editorial project that helps adult beginners make good, evidence-based decisions about learning piano, which app, which keyboard, how to practice, and whether they're "too old" (they aren't). Every factual claim links to a primary source: a peer-reviewed study, a manufacturer's spec sheet, or a vendor's own page.

How we research

  • Primary sources over hearsay. We cite the research (e.g. the PLoS One adult-plasticity study, the UCL 66-day habit study) and manufacturer specs (Roland, Yamaha) directly.
  • We don't invent numbers. When a vendor hides its pricing behind a signup flow, we say so rather than quote a figure that would be wrong. When a claim can't be verified, we describe it qualitatively.
  • We compare honestly. Our tables include the full range of credible options for a given goal, and we point you to whichever one is the better fit, even when it isn't the app we favor.

Disclosure

How we make moneyDisclosure: this site features commercial partners and promoted placements, which can influence which products appear and how they are ranked. Corrections

Specs, prices and app features change. If you spot something out of date or wrong, we want to fix it, the whole point of the site is that it stays accurate and citable.
