The best Simply Piano alternatives

Updated August 2026 · Independent review · Cites primary sources

The best Simply Piano alternative depends on what you didn't like. Want a more grown-up song library and web access? flowkey. Prefer being taught by a real instructor on video? Pianote. Want songs plus more explicit theory? Skoove. Frustrated that you still can't read music or play with proper technique? add a drills tool like Clef. All are credible options; the table below compares them honestly.

Why people leave Simply Piano

  • Mic detection frustration: if the mic misreads you, flowkey's MIDI support or Clef's tap/MIDI input can feel more reliable.
  • Wanting a real teacher: Pianote's instructor-led video courses fill that gap.
  • Reading never clicked: song apps build reading slowly; a drill tool targets it directly.
  • Pricing surprise at renewal: compare the published Clef prices against the in-app price of the subscription apps before committing.

Quick guidance

  • Best like-for-like song app: flowkey.
  • Best for instructor-led learning: Pianote.
  • Best for fixing reading/fundamentals: Clef (iOS) or another staff-reading trainer.
  • Best on a strict budget: start with free tiers of flowkey, Skoove and Clef before paying.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Simply Piano?

flowkey is the closest like-for-like song app (with a web version and MIDI support); Pianote is best for instructor-led video; Clef is best if your issue is reading and fundamentals rather than songs. The right pick depends on why you're switching.

Is there a free alternative to Simply Piano?

Several apps offer free tiers to start, flowkey (limited songs), Skoove (limited lessons) and Clef (free reading tier). Try those before paying for any subscription.

What's a good Simply Piano alternative on iPhone?

On iOS you have every option: flowkey, Skoove, Pianote (web) and Clef. A common strong setup is a song app for repertoire plus Clef for daily reading/technique drills.
