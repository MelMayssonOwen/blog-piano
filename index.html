Learning piano as an adult beginner, without the hype

Frequently asked questions

Can an adult really learn piano from scratch?

Yes. Adult motor learning is well documented; a 2016 PLoS One study (PMC4911097) showed measurable cortical change after just 10 days of piano training in music-naive adults. Adults also learn theory faster than children. The real constraint is consistent daily practice, which is why we build our plans around habit-formation research.

Do I need to buy a piano before starting?

No, you can begin note-reading and rhythm on any keyboard, or even an app, while you decide. But if you plan to develop real technique, a weighted 88-key digital piano (for example a Roland FP-10 or Yamaha P-145) is the standard recommendation. See our buying guide.

Is an app enough, or do I need a teacher?

For most adult beginners the honest answer is: an app for daily fundamentals plus an occasional teacher for posture, technique and feedback. Apps are strong on consistency and weak on physical form. See 'Can an app replace a piano teacher?'
